> A curated list of camera apps, editors, color tools and learning resources for taking better photos on iPhone.

apps and resources that actually help, grouped by the job they do. Entries are
alphabetical within each section, with a short note on who each one is for.

## Camera apps

### Composition and guided shooting

Apps that help you decide *where the frame goes*, not just how it is exposed.

- [Apple Camera](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/take-photos-iph263472f78/ios) - The built-in camera. Turn on *Settings › Camera › Grid* and *Level* for free rule-of-thirds lines and a horizon level.
- [Camera Guide](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/camera-guide/id6760614136) - 25 geometric composition overlays, including golden ratio and phi grid, for a one-time purchase.
- [slay](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/slay-ai-composition-camera/id6807513456) - 14 composition guides on the live viewfinder with the camera height, angle, zoom and distance for each; on-device subject tracking locks when the shot lines up. Free, with an optional Pro plan. *(made by us)*
- [Wise Camera](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/wise-camera/id1406085025) - A library of classic composition rules overlaid on the camera.

### Manual and pro cameras

For control over focus, exposure, RAW and lenses.

- [Blackmagic Camera](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/blackmagic-camera/id6449580241) - Free cinema-style camera with pro video controls from Blackmagic Design.
- [Halide Mark III](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/halide-mark-iii-pro-camera/id885697368) - Fast manual camera with RAW, manual focus and focus peaking.
- [Leica LUX](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/leica-lux-pro-manual-camera/id6477182657) - Manual camera with Leica looks and lens simulations.
- [Moment Pro Camera II](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/moment-pro-camera-ii/id6748837351) - Manual photo and video camera, built to pair with Moment lenses.
- [Obscura](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/obscura-pro-camera/id1579306989) - Dial-driven manual camera with a tactile interface and filters.
- [ProCam](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/procam-pro-camera/id730712409) - Many shooting modes in one app: manual, night, burst, portrait and more.
- [ProCamera](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/procamera-professional-camera/id694647259) - Long-running manual camera with RAW and HDR modes.

### Film look and point-and-shoot

Bake a look into the photo at capture.

- [mood.camera](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mood-camera/id6472954208) - Point-and-shoot that applies a film look to every photo.
- [Spectre Camera](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/spectre-camera/id1450074595) - Computational long exposures: smooth water, light trails, empty crowds.

### Video

- [Blackmagic Camera](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/blackmagic-camera/id6449580241) - See [above](#manual-and-pro-cameras).
- [Kino](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kino-pro-video-camera/id6472380172) - Cinematic video camera with built-in grades, from the makers of Halide.

## Editing apps

- [Darkroom](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/darkroom-photo-video-editor/id953286746) - Photo and video editor built around the Apple Photos library, with curves and color tools.
- [Lightroom](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lightroom-ai-photo-editor/id878783582) - Adobe's editor for RAW development, masking and presets, synced across devices.
- [Snapseed](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/snapseed-photo-editor-camera/id439438619) - Free editor from Google with selective adjustments, healing and perspective fixes.
- [VSCO](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vsco-photo-video-editor/id588013838) - Film-inspired presets and editing tools with a community feed.

## LUTs and color

- [slay LUTs](https://github.com/slaycamera/luts) - Five free cinematic `.cube` LUTs for CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro and Photoshop. CC BY 4.0. *(made by us)*

## Learn composition

Reference articles:

- [Golden ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_ratio) - The math behind the golden spiral and phi grid.
- [Leading lines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leading_lines) - Lines that pull the eye toward the subject.
- [Rule of thirds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rule_of_thirds) - The grid behind most balanced photos, and where it came from.
- [Vanishing point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanishing_point) - How converging lines create depth.

Practical guides:

- [Composition guides](https://github.com/slaycamera/composition-guides) - 14 composition recipes for portrait, travel and food photos with camera height, angle, zoom and distance, plus diagrams and JSON/CSV data. CC BY 4.0. *(made by us)*
- [Complete guide to photo composition](https://www.slaycamera.com/blog/complete-guide-to-photo-composition?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=awesome-iphone-photography) - Every major rule with when to use it and when to break it. *(made by us)*
- [How to use the iPhone camera grid](https://www.slaycamera.com/blog/how-to-use-iphone-camera-grid?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=awesome-iphone-photography) - Turn on the grid and actually use it. *(made by us)*
- [Golden ratio vs rule of thirds](https://www.slaycamera.com/blog/golden-ratio-vs-rule-of-thirds-iphone?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=awesome-iphone-photography) - When each grid works better. *(made by us)*
- [Common iPhone composition mistakes](https://www.slaycamera.com/blog/iphone-photography-composition-mistakes?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=awesome-iphone-photography) - The fixes that make the biggest difference. *(made by us)*
